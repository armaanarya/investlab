## Trading

- **$300,000** virtual cash, one shared WInS account. (Last season $500,000;
  StockTrak boilerplate says $100,000. Both wrong.)
- The $150,000 2028 contribution in the case is **not** added to WInS. WInS
  gains and losses are **not** carried into Laura's projections.
- U.S. orders during 9:30-4:00 ET fill at real-time prices; orders placed while
  closed fill at the next open. Displayed position prices lag 10-15 minutes.
  International stocks clear at the end of the next trading day, with automatic
  currency conversion. Bonds price once a day at the U.S. open and pay coupons
  every six months.
- **Up to 200 trades.** An order may not exceed **twice the security's current
  daily volume**.
- Commission: **$25** per stock trade, **$10** per Treasury bond trade, charged
  only on trades that clear. Dividends, splits and coupons are applied.
- Eligible: cash; stocks on any WInS exchange at **$5 or more** (or local
  equivalent); **any ETF available on WInS** (no Approved List this season);
  government bonds on WInS from the **US, UK, Germany, France, Italy,
  Netherlands**.
- Banned: margin, short selling, stock-secured debt, crypto, derivatives
  (options, futures, swaps), anything else.
- No required sector allocation. Diversification is judged, not mandated.
- The FAQ says teams "must meet the required trading activity and portfolio
  management guidelines"; no numeric minimum is published beyond what the
  Trading Notes Analysis implies (at least 3 executed trades by Oct 23).

## Tool readings (not stated in the rules)

- **Leveraged and inverse ETFs** are blocked. "Any ETF" is allowed, but
  derivatives are banned and these funds are built from swaps and futures.
- **Commodity ETFs such as GLD are allowed** here (they are ETFs, and
  commodities are not on the banned list). Different from DECA.
- **Crypto ETFs such as IBIT are banned** (crypto is named).
- The volume check uses the last completed session's volume, since the current
  day's is not known before the open.

## Judging

WInS returns, ranking and trade count are **not** judged. The three deliverables
(Trading Notes Analysis, IPS, Final Report) are evaluated together on five
criteria: Investment Strategy; Client Knowledge and Objectives; Portfolio
Analysis; Articulation of Competition Experience; Creativity and Presentation.
The criteria name the operating reserve, the facility contribution, funding
reliability "under varying market outcomes", Laura's residency goals, and the
credibility of the range quoted to co-sponsors.

**IPS format:** title page (team name, members as "First Name, Last Initial",
WInS username), then ≤2 pages: elevator pitch ≤50 words and IPS ≤500 words.
Times New Roman 12, double-spaced, 1-inch margins, PDF ≤5 MB. No graphics,
charts, links, footnotes or citations. Strategy only; no final reserve numbers
or projections (those belong in the Final Report).

## The client (numbers the tool encodes in `wharton_client.py`)

2026 = Year 0. Start 2027: $300,000. Start 2028: +$150,000. No other flows
before 2033. Start of 2033: set aside an operating reserve for ten fixed
$50,000 payments, start of 2033 through 2042, funded "with a high degree of
certainty" by the portfolio alone. The team defines that certainty and how it
was evaluated. Then recommend a facility contribution from what remains while
preserving flexibility. In 2031, Laura quotes co-sponsors a range with a stated
confidence; the team drafts part of that communication. Taxes and Taiwan
legal matters are out of scope; inflation assumptions are the team's.

## Authorship

Trading Notes "must correspond to trades executed in WInS. Yes, we will verify
this." The deliverables are evaluated on "an authentic team voice". The
2025-26 rules also said AI output may not be submitted as the team's own work
and AI use must be cited; the 2026-27 General Rules page could not be fetched
(403), so **re-read it** and assume that policy still holds. The tool gives
numbers; the team writes every note and every sentence it submits.
