## What exactly is due

### October 9: Official Team Roster

Submit the roster in SurveyMonkey Apply by 5:00 p.m. ET. It must include:

1. Full name of every student.
2. A unique email address for every student.
3. Birthday of every student: month, day, and year.
4. Anticipated graduation year of every student.
5. Official team name.
6. School country.
7. School state for a U.S. school.
8. School city.
9. Official school website URL.

The team must have four to six students from the same school. After submission,
the team may not add members. Removing a member requires the advisor to obtain
written approval from the competition, and the team must remain within the
four-to-six-member limit. The official team name and finalized members must
match the later IPS title page.

### October 23: Trading Notes Analysis

Submit the analysis through the SurveyMonkey Apply form by 5:00 p.m. ET. There
is no separate PDF, page count, or general essay specified for this
deliverable. The form requires **three Trading Notes from trades the team
actually executed in WInS**.

For each of the three decisions, submit:

1. The Trading Note exactly as it appears in WInS.
2. A separate reflection of **100 words or fewer** explaining:
   - why the team made the investment decision;
   - how the decision aligned with the overall investment strategy; and
   - how it supported Laura's goals, funding needs, or risk considerations.

Wharton verifies the selected notes against executed WInS trades. A selected
investment does not need to remain in the portfolio on November 6. The point is
to show how the decision supported, tested, or refined one cohesive developing
strategy. Do not put final reserve calculations, long-term projections, a final
facility-contribution range, or co-sponsor language here; those belong in the
Final Report.

Operationally, write a complete Trading Note in WInS when every trade is made,
even though only three are selected for this submission. That preserves an
honest contemporaneous record and gives the team real choices on October 23.

### November 6: Investment Policy Statement

Submit one PDF through SurveyMonkey Apply by 5:00 p.m. ET. The PDF is at most
**three pages** and **5 MB**.

**Page 1: Title Page, one page maximum**

- Official team name, matching the October 9 roster.
- Finalized team members, matching the roster, formatted as `First Name, Last
  Initial`.
- WInS username.

**Pages 2-3: Investment Strategy, two pages maximum**

- Investment Strategy Elevator Pitch: **50 words maximum**.
- Investment Policy Statement: **500 words maximum**.

The elevator pitch states the strategy's central idea and how it supports
Laura's goals and future funding needs. The IPS explains:

- what the team is trying to accomplish for Laura;
- why the strategy fits her goals, time horizons, and needs;
- the approach to risk and return, liquidity, diversification, and portfolio
  construction;
- how the asset allocation will change as funding dates approach and payments
  are made;
- how the strategy prepares for and protects the ten-year operating
  commitment;
- how the operating reserve's composition should change over time;
- the framework for choosing a responsible facility contribution; and
- how the strategy preserves financial flexibility and handles uncertainty.

Required format: Times New Roman, 12-point, double-spaced, one-inch margins.
Graphics, charts, images, attachments, external links, footnotes, and formal
citations are prohibited. The IPS states the strategy and decision framework.
It should not include final reserve calculations, detailed projections, a
final facility-contribution range, or draft co-sponsor communication.

At the deadline, three things lock at once: the IPS becomes the official
strategy, WInS trading ends, and the portfolio freezes. The Final Report may
evaluate mistakes or explain planned changes already built into the strategy,
but it may not replace the IPS with a redesigned strategy after seeing market
results.

### November 9: Final Report instructions released

Nothing is submitted on November 9. Wharton will publish the detailed page
limit, file-size limit, layout, citation, and other formatting rules for the
Final Report. Do not borrow those details from a prior season. Add the new
requirements to this file as soon as Wharton releases them.

### December 4: Final Report and school documentation

Submit both items through SurveyMonkey Apply by 5:00 p.m. ET. They are separate
files.

**Final Report.** The report must evaluate how the team implemented the locked
IPS strategy and present its analysis and recommendations for Laura. The
current guide and case study require the report to address:

- how the WInS portfolio decisions implemented the IPS;
- the recommended size and initial asset composition of the operating reserve
  at the beginning of 2033;
- how the reserve composition should change as the ten annual $50,000 payments
  approach and are made from 2033 through 2042;
- the team's definition of a high degree of funding certainty, how that level
  was evaluated, and the assumptions behind it;
- the amount Laura can responsibly contribute to the residency facility after
  establishing the reserve;
- the financial flexibility she should keep after that contribution;
- the dollar range Laura should communicate to co-sponsors in 2031, the team's
  confidence that the 2033 contribution will fall in that range, and how
  favorable and unfavorable markets affect it;
- a draft portion of Laura's fundraising communication to prospective
  co-sponsors; and
- assumptions about returns, risk, cash-flow timing, inflation, outside
  funding, and facility costs, supported by relevant evidence and sources.

The report is judged on investment strategy, client knowledge and objectives,
portfolio analysis, the team's competition experience, and creativity and
presentation. Exact page, file, format, and citation requirements are **not yet
published**. They arrive November 9 and must be added here before report work is
formatted.

**Official school documentation.** Submit the school document as a separate
file. The General Rules say it must be on official school letterhead and
confirm that the team has permission to participate and that all students and
the advisor are affiliated with the school. Request it early. The deliverables
page says a sample is forthcoming, so verify the final template after Wharton
publishes it.

### After December 4: advancement rounds

Wharton selects the top 50 semifinalists based on the IPS and Final Reports,
provided the teams met all eligibility, trading, and submission requirements.
Semifinalists prepare a live virtual presentation about their strategy,
analysis, and ten-week experience; every team member must participate. The top
10 advance to the April 29-30 Global Finale. Wharton has not yet published the
semifinal announcement date or virtual presentation dates.

## Trading

- **$300,000** virtual cash, one shared WInS account. (Last season $500,000;
  StockTrak boilerplate says $100,000. Both wrong.)
- The $150,000 2028 contribution in the case is **not** added to WInS. WInS
  gains and losses are **not** carried into Laura's projections.
- U.S. orders during 9:30-4:00 ET fill at real-time prices; orders placed while
  closed fill at the next open. Displayed position prices lag 10-15 minutes.
  International stocks clear at the end of the next trading day, with automatic
  currency conversion. Bonds price once a day at the U.S. open and pay coupons
  every six months.
- **Up to 200 trades.** An order may not exceed **twice the security's current
  daily volume**.
- Commission: **$25** per stock trade, **$10** per Treasury bond trade, charged
  only on trades that clear. Dividends, splits and coupons are applied.
- Eligible: cash; stocks on any WInS exchange at **$5 or more** (or local
  equivalent); **any ETF available on WInS** (no Approved List this season);
  government bonds on WInS from the **US, UK, Germany, France, Italy,
  Netherlands**.
- Banned: margin, short selling, stock-secured debt, crypto, derivatives
  (options, futures, swaps), anything else.
- No required sector allocation. Diversification is judged, not mandated.
- The FAQ says teams "must meet the required trading activity and portfolio
  management guidelines"; no numeric minimum is published beyond what the
  Trading Notes Analysis implies (at least 3 executed trades by Oct 23).

## Tool readings (not stated in the rules)

- **Leveraged and inverse ETFs** are blocked. "Any ETF" is allowed, but
  derivatives are banned and these funds are built from swaps and futures.
- **Commodity ETFs such as GLD are allowed** here (they are ETFs, and
  commodities are not on the banned list). Different from DECA.
- **Crypto ETFs such as IBIT are banned** (crypto is named).
- The volume check uses the last completed session's volume, since the current
  day's is not known before the open.

## Judging

WInS returns, ranking and trade count are **not** judged. The three deliverables
(Trading Notes Analysis, IPS, Final Report) are evaluated together on five
criteria: Investment Strategy; Client Knowledge and Objectives; Portfolio
Analysis; Articulation of Competition Experience; Creativity and Presentation.
The criteria name the operating reserve, the facility contribution, funding
reliability "under varying market outcomes", Laura's residency goals, and the
credibility of the range quoted to co-sponsors.

## The client (numbers the tool encodes in `wharton_client.py`)

2026 = Year 0. Start 2027: $300,000. Start 2028: +$150,000. No other flows
before 2033. Start of 2033: set aside an operating reserve for ten fixed
$50,000 payments, start of 2033 through 2042, funded "with a high degree of
certainty" by the portfolio alone. The team defines that certainty and how it
was evaluated. Then recommend a facility contribution from what remains while
preserving flexibility. In 2031, Laura quotes co-sponsors a range with a stated
confidence; the team drafts part of that communication. Taxes and Taiwan
legal matters are out of scope; inflation assumptions are the team's.

## Authorship

The 2026-27 General Rules permit generative AI for brainstorming and idea
generation. AI-generated work may not be submitted as the team's own. Any
AI-generated material included in a report must be cited like another source.
Plagiarism or misuse of AI falls under the competition's ethics and academic
integrity rules.

Use the tool and AI assistance for facts, calculations, models, research paths,
questions, and critique. The students make the decisions and write the final
Trading Notes, reflections, IPS, report, and presentation in their own
authentic voice.
